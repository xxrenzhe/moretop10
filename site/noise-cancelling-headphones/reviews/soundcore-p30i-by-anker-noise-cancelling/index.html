Day-two ownership

In everyday use, the P30i is straightforward but requires sensible maintenance. The rear hinge that forms the phone stand is a mechanical moving part; keeping it free of pocket lint and avoiding excessive backward force will ensure it remains stable when holding up heavier phones.

Because the earbuds emphasize strong low-end response, keeping the ear tips clean is critical. Debris accumulation on the 10mm driver grilles can degrade volume output and compromise the seal needed for the 42dB adaptive noise cancellation to work properly. While the 45-hour reservoir reduces how often you need a wall outlet, keep in mind that real-world battery life varies based on volume levels and whether ANC or Transparency Mode is continuously active.
