As of 2026-10-22, Samsung Tizen generally cannot run the unofficial launcher path this cluster documents for Android TV / Google TV. There is no supported ADB / sideload route. Settings can mute some prompts. They will not replace the home row. If you already own a different set, start on how to remove smart TV ads. If you are replacing this Samsung, use when software will not install. We will not invent a Samsung Check price button. A Fire Stick is not a Tizen workaround; we will not recommend one.

Why ADB and sideload usually fail

Tizen is not Android. Developer options, USB debugging, and community APKs that work on Google TV do not apply here. Forum “ADB on Samsung” threads are usually about a phone, a leftover Android-based set from years ago, or a hack that does not survive a firmware update. We will not publish those steps. The unofficial CleanYourTV project is third-party open-source and non-official — and it is written for Android TV / Google TV, not Tizen.

  • No supported USB-debugging surface for a third-party home app.
  • Vendor firmware can wipe unofficial packages on reboot or update.
  • Samsung’s home row, Art Store promos, and Smart Hub tiles are part of the OS, not an extra APK you can hide with ADB.

What settings can still do

You can still reduce some noise without pretending sideload works: turn off automatic content recognition where the menu exists, decline interest-based ads, skip optional Samsung accounts you do not need, and mute “recommended” rows that have a toggle. Boot splash and Smart Hub ads usually stay. Ads inside Netflix or YouTube are app subscriptions — a new television will not remove them.

If ADB “not working” is why you landed here, read ADB troubleshooting only after you confirm the set is actually Android / Google TV. On Tizen, that page does not apply.

Honest next steps

  1. Confirm the OS on the sticker or in Support / About. If it says Tizen, stop looking for a sideload.
  2. Use settings for prompts you can actually turn off. Do not jailbreak the set.
  3. If the frustration is launcher ads and you are willing to change platforms, open when software will not install. Primary links there are Google TV whole TVs — not a Fire Stick, not a projector in the hero row.
  4. If you want the OS gone, read non-smart TVs. If the goal is accounts and ACR, read TVs for privacy. Both stay content-only until a real track exists.
  5. If someone in the house has an Android / Google TV, send them to Google TV ads instead of this page.
