As of 2026-10-22, start on the software guide if the TV is Android or Google TV. Come here when install failed, ADB is missing, or you are replacing a Samsung / LG set. Primary recommendations below are Hisense Google TV whole TVs — not projectors, not Fire sticks, not HDMI hubs. Fire TV panels are listed separately so nobody treats them as an “ad-free” upgrade. Decision tree

  1. Android TV / Google TV, ADB works — stay on the software path. See Google TV ads. Do not buy another TV to solve a launcher problem.
  2. Android TV / Google TV, ADB broken — read ADB not working. If it stays broken, a Google TV replacement is a last resort, not a first click.
  3. Samsung Tizen or LG webOS — there is no unofficial sideload path. Read Samsung or LG first. A Google TV whole set is the honest hardware alternative we can link today.
  4. Fire TV panel you already own — try Fire software first on Fire TV panels. Do not buy a second Fire TV to escape ads. The Fire list below is for people who already want that OS, with the limitation stated.
  5. Want a box or a non-smart panel — Apple TV, onn, and privacy / non-smart sets are listed as empty slots until a real track exists. Read when a non-smart TV is the honest answer and privacy-oriented TV buying before you shop.
