Day-two ownership

Long-term satisfaction with any wet-dry vacuum hinges on how much manual scrubbing it demands after the job is finished. The F25 XT automates this through its self-cleaning dock. Once docked, the base initiates bi-directional roller scrubbing to dislodge caked mud and deep-set residue, followed by high-temperature drying at 194°F. With two drying modes available, owners can choose between faster cycles or quieter maintenance depending on household routines.

Day-to-day handling also benefits from chassis balance: the lower-mounted water tank keeps operational weight close to the floor, reducing the wrist torque required to steer the 70-degree articulating neck around furniture. How does the F25 XT handle hair and water management?

Water streaks and roller wrap are common failure points on standard floor washers. The roborock F25 XT addresses both using an internal dual-scraper mechanism. As the roller spins against the floor, a rigid comb continuously pulls pet and human hair away from the fibers before it can bundle around the axle.

Simultaneously, a flat blade squeegees dirty water directly into the high-pressure recovery channel powered by the 20,000 Pa motor. This configuration prevents pooled dirty water from redepositing across clean planks, speeding up ambient floor drying after each pass.
