How does the F25 GT Gen 2 handle tough spills and pet hair?

The F25 GT Gen 2 pairs 20,000 Pa suction with a 450 RPM spinning roller to lift kitchen grease, spilled cereal, bathroom grime, and pet hair in a single pass. Rather than pushing dirty water forward, the onboard system uses continuous water delivery while the dual edge scraper strips water from the roller to minimize streaks across hardwood, tile, and vinyl.

To combat pet hair wrap—a common failure point in entry-level floor washers—roborock built a curved comb over the roller chamber. This comb physically separates wet hair strands from the roller surface before they can wind tightly around the spindle, redirecting them into the recovery channel to prevent clogs.
