How does the F25 ACE Pro handle low furniture and baseboards?

Standard wet-dry vacs typically shut off suction or leak dirty water if tilted past a 45-degree angle. The F25 ACE Pro solves this using its FlatReach 2.0 architecture, which allows a full 180° lay-flat extension. At roughly 4.9 inches tall in this position, it slides beneath low bed frames, coffee tables, and sofas without cutting motor power or sacrificing suction performance.

For edge-to-edge detailing, the cleaner features three-sided brush coverage across the front, left, and right profiles of the head. Combined with a 70° swivel radius and a dynamic push-pull assist system that matches user arm motion, it tracks alongside kitchen islands and baseboards without leaving wide unwashed margins. A "Grab & Go" upright pause mode lets you stop cleaning instantly if you need to move a chair or clear an obstacle.

What role does the foam system play against tough messes?

Rather than spraying standard diluted water, this machine incorporates an Advanced Foam Cleaning System designed for kitchen grease, dining spills, bathroom film, and pet accidents. The system generates a clinging foam that applies across the floor in a three-step breakdown process, helping dissolve stubborn grime before the high-torque roller and downward pressure scrub the surface.

Backing up the foam action is a 25,000 Pa suction motor that channels slurry and dry grit into the recovery path in a single sweep. The roller features a specialized anti-tangle design to reduce hair binding during pet mess pick-ups, though exact maintenance intervals will depend on household shedding levels.
