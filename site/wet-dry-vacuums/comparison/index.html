FAQ

Can a wet-dry vacuum mop thick carpet?

No. These cordless vacuum-mop combos are for sealed hard floors—laminate, vinyl plank, and tile. Skip if you mainly have thick carpet.

Do I need a self-cleaning roller?

Self-clean and high-temp drying help after everyday spill cleanup so you are not rinsing a mop bucket. Confirm steam option, hard-floor fit, and tank emptying on the retailer page.

Do I still have to empty the dirty-water tank?

Yes. Heated drying does not remove the need to empty the dirty-water tank after use. Leaving wet sludge in the collection bin will cause odors regardless of roller drying.

When should I pick a wet-dry stick instead of a robot mop?

Choose a wet-dry combo for spill cleanup and sealed-floor mopping you want to do yourself. Skip it if you rarely mop—a stick vac alone may be enough—and skip steam models if you do not need steam.
