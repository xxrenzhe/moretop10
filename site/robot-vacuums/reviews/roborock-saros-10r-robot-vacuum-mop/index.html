How does the 3.14-inch profile clean low clearance spaces?

Traditional high-performance robot vacuums mount their laser rangefinders in a raised disc on top of the housing, instantly adding an inch or more to their vertical footprint. The Saros 10R reconfigures this layout into an ultra-thin 3.14-inch chassis powered by the StarSight Autonomous System 2.0.

By eliminating the top-heavy LDS housing, the unit passes underneath low furniture aprons where pet dander and dust bunnies tend to settle unnoticed. Navigation is handled through advanced 3D sensing and VertiBeam Lateral Obstacle Avoidance. This sensory suite monitors clearances horizontally and vertically, allowing the unit to navigate narrow-legged dining sets and base trim without repeatedly banging its chassis against furniture legs.

How do the FlexiArm systems tackle corners and edges?

Round robot chassis typically leave a faint perimeter of unswept grit along baseboards and right-angle room corners. The Saros 10R compensates mechanically using its FlexiArm Riser Side Brush and extending mop assembly.

When the lateral sensors detect an interior corner or an edge boundary, the side brush automatically extends outward from the chassis perimeter to flick debris into the 22,000 Pa suction path. Simultaneously, the mop extends to sweep moist microfiber closer to the baseboard than standard fixed circular pads can reach. If you have hard surfaces meeting raised transition strips or base trim, the arm mechanism flexes to track the structural perimeter.
