Day-two ownership

Living with the G2000 Pro involves balancing software customizability with desk footprint. On a Windows PC, downloading the HECATE software gives you full reign over the 7 lighting modes and activates the virtual 7.1 surround sound profile for competitive gaming. If you do not want software running in the background, the physical buttons on the housing let you change modes and toggle lighting directly.

Because the enclosure uses transparent sections to project its 270-degree glow from the 100 internal RGB beads, day-two maintenance requires keeping a microfiber cloth nearby to wipe down dust and fingerprints. Cable routing also requires a moment of planning: you will need to manage the tether between satellites, the wall power adapter, and your chosen USB or 3.5mm input line.

How does it handle multi-platform connectivity?

The G2000 Pro adapts well to mixed desks housing both a computer and a console. For PC and PS5 users, the USB-C interface offers an ultra-low latency pathway for direct digital audio. Xbox and Nintendo Switch players can connect using the 3.5mm AUX port.

Wireless streaming is managed through Bluetooth 5.4, which maintains a stable link up to 33 feet for phones and tablets. In the box, Edifier supplies:

  • A USB-C to USB-A cable
  • A 3.5mm auxiliary audio cable
  • A dedicated power adapter

If you need specialized optical or balanced XLR connections, check the retailer page specifications, as they are not part of this core loadout.

How is the acoustic hardware engineered?

Rather than using basic plastic shells, the G2000 Pro integrates MDF driver enclosures with scratch-resistant aluminum caps. The system relies on 3-inch full-range drivers supported by digital signal processing (DSP) to minimize distortion at elevated listening levels. To keep heavy bass passes from shaking your tabletop, the base of each satellite includes anti-slip isolation feet designed to absorb mechanical vibrations. Edifier also notes that the build has been tested for more than 500 hours under extreme temperature conditions.
