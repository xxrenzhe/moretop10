  • The Edifier M60 fits professionals, students, and home office users who want a clean, self-powered stereo setup without adding a separate receiver or DAC stack to their desk. Because it carries Hi-Res Audio certification alongside USB-C and Bluetooth V5.3, it suits users who alternate between a wired laptop dock during work hours and a wireless phone connection during breaks. Day-two ownership

Living with the Edifier M60 is relatively frictionless because it operates as an active, all-in-one system. There are no passive speaker wires to strip or external power bricks for secondary amplifiers to hide. Day-to-day maintenance primarily involves wiping dust from the matte black enclosures and keeping debris off the exposed driver cones. If you intend to run cables across a standing desk, verify your reach and port accessibility on the retailer listing, as included cable lengths vary by production batch.

How do the acoustic hardware and dimensions fit a tight workstation?

Desktop space is often constrained by multi-monitor arms, laptop stands, and peripherals. Edifier sizes each M60 cabinet to 100mm (W) x 168mm (H) x 147mm (D). Despite the small profile, the system outputs 66W RMS total power.

The audio architecture separates high and low frequencies cleanly rather than relying on a single full-range driver. Treble is handled by 1-inch silk dome tweeters rated at 15W per channel, while lower midrange punch is driven by 3-inch long-throw aluminum diaphragm drivers rated at 18W per channel. Supported by internal Class-D amplification, the M60 produces room-filling volume while remaining small enough to sit directly beneath or beside typical computer monitors.

How does the input selection handle daily switching?

Rather than forcing users into a single proprietary interface, the M60 provides three distinct connection options:

  • USB-C: Delivers a direct digital link to modern laptops, desktop PCs, or tablets, taking advantage of the unit's onboard digital processing.
  • AUX: Accommodates standard analog outputs from legacy PCs, monitors, or audio interfaces.
  • Bluetooth V5.3: Provides low-friction wireless streaming from phones and tablets with modern wireless protocol stability.

This multi-input arrangement lets you leave a primary workstation permanently wired to the USB-C or AUX terminal while leaving the Bluetooth channel open for quick audio playback from secondary mobile devices.
