Day-two ownership and routine maintenance

Living with this machine involves managing thermal routines and standard cleanings:

  • Preheat protocol: According to manufacturer guidelines, normal operation requires an initial preheat time of 60 to 80 seconds.
  • Cool-down cycles: After steaming milk, the boiler remains at high steaming temperatures. The manufacturer advises running a hot water cycle (without coffee in the portafilter) to accelerate system cooling before pulling another espresso shot.
  • Cleaning components: Daily upkeep centers on the detachable drip tray and the removable rear water reservoir. Both pull away from the chassis for quick sink rinsing. The steam wand must be wiped and purged immediately after milk texturing to prevent dried milk buildup inside the tip.
  • Warranty protection: Gevi backs units bought through official channels with a 12-month warranty and lifetime tech support.

How does the brew system handle extraction control?

The Gevi unit combines a 20-bar pump with internal NTC temperature control and a pre-infusion step. Pre-infusion dampens the coffee puck with low pressure before ramping up, helping reduce channeling and encouraging an even extraction across the coffee grounds.

For output, the control panel offers three brewing selections:

  • One Shot: Programmed for quick single extractions.
  • Double Shot: Calibrated for standard double-shot volumes.
  • Manual Control: Allows users to manually start and stop the pump to tailor brew strength and extraction ratio to personal taste.
