Day-two ownership and routine maintenance

Owning a semi-automatic machine with a steam wand introduces daily maintenance steps that buyers should anticipate:

  • Wand hygiene: Because the steam wand is submerged directly into milk, it requires an immediate wipe with a damp cloth and a brief steam purge after every single use to prevent milk residue from baking onto the metal tip.
  • Portafilter cleaning: You will need to knock out spent espresso pucks and rinse the portafilter basket after each extraction cycle to prevent stale coffee oils from altering subsequent brews.
  • Water management: Due to its larger tank profile, checking water levels is less frequent than on micro-sized units, but regular water refreshes and descaling cycles remain mandatory to protect the internal 1350W heating system from mineral buildup.
