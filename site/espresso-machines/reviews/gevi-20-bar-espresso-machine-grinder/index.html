Who it is for

  • The Gevi 20-Bar machine is designed for home baristas who prioritize an uncluttered countertop and refuse to buy a standalone grinder. If you want freshly ground beans delivered straight into your portafilter before pulling a shot, this all-in-one chassis eliminates the logistical headache of pairing two separate machines. Its 30 grind settings allow you to step smoothly from ultra-fine espresso to coarser textures without sacrificing the convenience of single or double volumetric shot presets.

Who should skip

  • Skip this machine if your equipment budget sits under roughly $200, or if your kitchen counter already houses a dependable, standalone burr grinder. Day-two ownership

Living with an integrated grinder means maintenance extends beyond wiping down the drip tray. To maintain consistent shots across all 30 settings, the conical burrs require routine brushing to prevent ground retention and coffee oil accumulation.

For milk drinks, the steam wand serves a practical dual purpose: it produces microfoam for lattes and cappuccinos, but turning the control knob also routes plain hot water through the nozzle. This feature is handy for quickly making an Americano or purging milk residue immediately after steaming, keeping daily upkeep friction low.

Why choose an all-in-one chassis over a wand-only machine?

The defining reason to choose the Gevi is the pairing of a conical burr mechanism directly alongside a 20-bar extraction system. Most entry-level wand machines force you to either purchase pre-ground coffee—which limits extraction quality—or spend extra money and counter real estate on an external grinder.

With 30 distinct grind settings and adjustable grinding duration, Gevi allows you to control the exact dose and fineness needed for a balanced extraction.
