FAQ: buying ceramic space heaters

Will a 1500W ceramic heater cover a large room?

The Premium card on this shortlist claims 270 sq.ft coverage with 70° oscillation. Compact bedside units are written for bedrooms and desks. Confirm wattage and claimed coverage on the retailer page before you buy.

Do I need 70° oscillation?

Atom One and the Premium large-room card list 70° wide-angle oscillation for garages, basements, and offices. The compact white and Gold cards do not list that sweep. Skip oscillation if you only want a quiet bedside heater.

When should I skip a compact ceramic heater?

Skip the compact white or Gold cards if you need garage or 270 sq.ft coverage. Those listings are the quiet 34dB / 1500W remote stack for bedrooms and offices, not a large-room default.

What should I confirm on the listing?

Confirm wattage, oscillation, noise, tip-over protection, and that you can plug directly into a wall outlet — listings warn against extension cords.
