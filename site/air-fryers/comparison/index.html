FAQ

What size air fryer do I need for weeknight dinners?

6–6.8 qt single-basket models cover most weeknight crisping without heating the whole oven. TwinFry’s 9 qt cavity with a removable divider is for two timings at once. Skip it if counter depth is tight.

Do I need dual heating or TurboBlaze?

Dual heating cooks from the top and bottom so you shake less. TurboBlaze listings cite a high-speed fan and temperatures up to 450°F. Confirm basket capacity, counter depth, and footprint on the retailer page.

When should I skip a dual-zone air fryer?

Skip a 9 qt double basket if you only cook one food at a time, or if you only want the cheapest ceramic basket that works.
