How do the Sync Finish and Sync Cook modes work together?

The primary advantage of the COSORI Dual Air Fryer lies in how its heating zones are controlled. The Sync Finish function is designed for disparate foods. If your vegetables require 12 minutes at 375°F while chicken thighs need 22 minutes at 400°F, Sync Finish holds the vegetable basket until the exact moment needed so both zones shut off simultaneously.

Conversely, the Sync Cook mode is designed for duplicate meals. Instead of programming basket one and manually matching the inputs on basket two, pressing Sync Cook clones your chosen function, temperature, and duration across the entire 9-quart capacity instantly.
