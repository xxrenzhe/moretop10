How does the divider and dual-zone timing work?

The central feature of the TwinFry is its dual-purpose chamber. When the divider is inserted, the unit functions as two 4.5-quart air fryers side-by-side.

To manage different foods, the appliance relies on two automated synchronization features:

  • Sync function: You can program two completely different temperatures and cooking times (such as proteins on one side and delicate vegetables on the other), and the machine holds the faster item until the correct moment so both compartments complete their cycles simultaneously.
  • Match function: If you are cooking a high volume of the same item across both 4.5-quart sections, Match duplicates your settings across both zones with a single input.

Removing the partition converts the chamber into a single 9-quart basket. This eliminates the central wall entirely, giving you the uninterrupted horizontal floor area needed for larger cuts of meat or full meals that would otherwise require batch cooking.
