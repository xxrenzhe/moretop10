How do the NeoSight and TripleStrike features impact daily cleaning?

Most stick vacuums rely on forward-facing white LEDs that merely illuminate the room rather than highlighting fine grit. The VortexIQ Pro's NeoSight Dust Detection casts a high-contrast green LED light across a 150° beam. By projecting light at ground level, particles cast distinct shadows—making debris appear up to 12x larger so you can quickly see whether a path requires another pass.

Complementing this is the Anti-Tangle TripleStrike roller brush. Traditional rollers trap long pet and human hair around the central spindle, requiring scissors or utility blades to clear. LEVOIT’s patented roller geometry guides strands directly off the brush and straight into the airflow path, preventing wraps before the hair reaches the dust bin.
