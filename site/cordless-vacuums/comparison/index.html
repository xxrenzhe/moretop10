FAQ

Do I need an auto-empty dock on a cordless stick vac?

Only if you hate emptying a bin and have floor space for a dock tower. Mid-chart sticks such as VortexIQ Pro lead when you want strong suction and anti-tangle without committing to an auto-empty dock.

Will a cordless stick handle carpet and hard floors?

Yes—these are stick vacuums for quick carpet and hard-floor pickups, no robot required. Confirm runtime, bin emptying, and dock space on the retailer page.

How much runtime do I need?

Skip a lower-priced anti-tangle stick if you need 60+ minute runtime or auto-empty. Confirm listed runtime on the retailer page against your floor plan.
