FAQ

Battery or wired video doorbell?

Battery models install wire-free and list up to about 10 months on a charge. Wired units need a compatible transformer. Confirm power type, field of view, and kit contents on the retailer page.

Do I need dual cameras for packages?

E340 leads for porch packages: dual cameras (face-on plus downward package view), 2K, and onboard storage. Skip it if you need HomeKit Secure Video as a hard requirement.

Does a video doorbell require a subscription?

Several picks advertise onboard or hub storage with no mandatory monthly plan. Confirm local storage and any optional cloud plan on the retailer page.
