Battery vs. hardwired: How does the power setup work?

The eufy Security Video Doorbell C31 is built around a quick-release 6,500mAh rechargeable battery, allowing users to mount it completely wire-free. However, the hardware also supports direct connection to existing doorbell wiring.

When hardwiring, the 6,500mAh battery must remain inserted inside the doorbell housing to maintain operation. Connecting to low-voltage transformer wiring unlocks continuous 24/7 recording capabilities as well as a 5-second pre-roll buffer on motion-activated events. For safety during installation or battery swaps, power must be cut off at the circuit breaker.
