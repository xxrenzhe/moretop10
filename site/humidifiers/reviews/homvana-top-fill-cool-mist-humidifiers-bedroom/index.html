Day-two ownership

Because this Homvana model uses an ultrasonic, filter-free architecture, you save money over time by never buying replacement paper wicks. However, day-two maintenance requires routine owner attention. Mineral scale from tap water will settle directly into the 4L basin and ultrasonic plate over time.

Homvana offsets this maintenance burden through thoughtful accessibility: the top-fill design allows you to pour water in directly without taking the tank to the sink, the mist tube detaches completely, and an included cleaning brush helps scrub narrow channels. Wiping down the reservoir weekly and maintaining clean surfaces will ensure the 300ml/h mist channel remains clear and odor-free.

The open top-fill reservoir makes monitoring remaining water levels straightforward and lets you top off the basin using a household pitcher at any point during operation.
