How does the 4-way drainage system work in practice?

A major challenge in home dehumidification is emptying the reservoir before the machine stops running. Levoit addresses this by integrating four separate disposal pathways:

  1. Internal water tank: For standalone manual emptying in rooms without plumbing access.
  2. Gravity drain: Using standard passive gravity lines when placed near a low drain.
  3. Garden hose outlet: Using the included garden hose connector for routing water directly outdoors or into floor grates.
  4. Removable active pump: Capable of pumping vertical discharge up to 3 meters (9.8 feet) high.

Because the pump is removable and washable, it avoids the permanent sediment clogs that often ruin integrated pump units over multi-season use.

What protection does the MERV 11 filtration provide?

Standard dehumidifiers typically rely on coarse mesh screens that stop only hair and large lint. This Levoit model comes equipped with a MERV 11 filter designed to prevent finer dust buildup inside the internal coils and mechanical components. In addition to preserving the internal airflow dynamics across its 3-speed fan system, keeping the internal refrigeration system clear of dust helps sustain the machine's EPA Most Efficient 2025 energy profile over time.
