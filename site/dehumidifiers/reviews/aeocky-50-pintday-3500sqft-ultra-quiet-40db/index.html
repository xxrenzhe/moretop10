Day-two ownership: Maintenance and drainage friction

The biggest friction point in day-to-day operation is the onboard water tank capacity. With a high daily extraction rate of 50 pints against a small 0.8-gallon tank, manual bucket emptying becomes a constant chore during warm, humid months. If you intend to run the unit continuously on its internal tank alone, expect to empty the reservoir multiple times per day.

To achieve hands-off operation, treat this as a continuous-drain appliance from day one. Connecting the included drain hose bypasses the bucket, channeling water straight to a floor drain via gravity. Because the unit relies solely on gravity rather than an internal mechanical pump, your drainage line must maintain a downward slope along its entire run without kinks or elevation rises.
