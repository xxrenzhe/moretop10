Day-two ownership

Living with the Neakasa M1 Plus means keeping the mechanical pathway clear so the brush seal stays intact. Over time, litter dust or loose granules can accumulate around the bottom brush strip; keeping that contact point clean ensures the waste bin remains tightly sealed against odors.

Multi-cat homes (up to 3 cats) will need to monitor the waste bin collection frequency through regular checks or the Neakasa companion app. If you bring a new kitten home that weighs under 3.3 lbs, remember to manually toggle Kitten Mode in the app before allowing the pet near the automated box.

How does the open-top structure balance cat comfort with odor containment?

Most automatic litter boxes enclose cats inside a rotating barrel, which can deter anxious or large-breed cats. The Neakasa M1 Plus solves this by leaving the entry completely open, offering a visible space where cats feel unconfined and in control.

To control smells without enclosing the cat, the unit seals odors at the source beneath the litter tray. A built-in brush strip located at the bottom of the litter bed creates a tight mechanical seal between the rotating bed and the lower waste collection bin. Waste is deposited below and isolated from the open surface, preventing odors from continuously drafting up through the clean litter.

  3. App-managed safety: For households with kittens under 3.3 lbs, owners can engage Kitten Mode via the Neakasa App to adjust cycle behavior.
